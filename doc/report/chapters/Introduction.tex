\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
\noindent In this project, we analyze how early childhood education and care (ECEC) services influence women's participation in the labor market in Italian provinces, focusing on purely spatial areal data observed for a single year (2022). 
The goal is to detect spatial clusters that capture the joint variation in ECEC availability and women's employment outcomes. In particular, we perform model-based clustering by allowing regression coefficients to vary across latent mixture components, so that provinces are grouped according to similarities in the relationship between ECEC indicators and women's employment.\\

\noindent The analysis follows a structured pipeline\footnote{Code available at https://github.com/leonardotisato/Bayesian-Statistics-Project.}. We first describe the data sources and perform an exploratory analysis to summarize the geographic patterns of key variables. We then carry out a variable selection step to select the set of predictors that are most relevant for our analysis, aiming to reduce redundancy. Next, we perform label assignment via a Bayesian finite mixture model. Based on these assignments, we fit the model using only the selected covariates, with spatial random effect, and we evaluated it using posterior summaries and diagnostics (e.g., convergence checks and posterior predictive assessment).
